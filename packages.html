Ask a Question
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks ahead for standard packages, and 6–8 weeks ahead for weddings and large corporate events. Popular dates (weekends, holidays, milestone birthdays) fill quickly — the earlier you book, the more options you'll have.
Do you deliver and install, or do I pick up?
Every package includes delivery, installation, and teardown. Our team arrives 1–2 hours before your event, sets everything up beautifully, and returns after to clean up. You don't lift a finger.
Can I customize a package?
Absolutely — that's our favorite part. Every package is a starting point. We can add florals, swap color palettes, extend the balloon garland, or blend two packages together. Just tell us your vision and we'll send a custom quote within 48 hours.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
We offer free rescheduling up to 7 days before your event. Cancellations 7+ days out receive a full refund minus the deposit. Within 7 days, we can typically apply your deposit toward a future booking within 6 months.
How do you handle outdoor setups?
Outdoor events are our specialty — we use UV-resistant balloons, weighted bases, and weather-safe adhesives. In case of extreme wind or rain on the day, we'll coordinate a backup plan or move the setup to an indoor location at no extra charge.
Do you travel outside the city?
Yes — we serve 12 cities across the East Coast. Deliveries within a 30-mile radius of our studio are free. Beyond that, we add a small travel fee based on distance. Get in touch with your venue address for an exact quote.
