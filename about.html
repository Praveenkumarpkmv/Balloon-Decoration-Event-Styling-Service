Our Story

From One Balloon
To 500 Events.

BalloonAura started in 2019 with a single balloon arch for a friend's birthday. What began as a weekend hobby quickly grew into a full-service event styling studio trusted by hundreds of families, brands, and wedding planners.

We still design every project like it's our first — with fresh eyes, obsessive attention to color theory, and the belief that celebrations deserve to feel extraordinary, not templated.

Today our team operates across 12 cities, delivering everything from intimate in-home setups to full-scale corporate galas — all under one roof.

Our Values

What We Stand For.

Three principles that guide every install, every moodboard, every mood.

Craft Over Volume

We take on a limited number of events each month so every project gets our full creative energy — never a rushed, templated setup.

Always On Time

If we say we'll be there at 10am, we're there at 9:45. Punctuality is the most underrated form of respect in our industry.

Sustainability First

Where possible we use biodegradable latex, reusable props, and eco-conscious florals. Celebrating shouldn't cost the planet.
