Our Process

From Concept to
Celebration.

We make booking event decor entirely stress-free. Here is exactly what you can expect when you work with BalloonAura.

Consultation

Step 02

Design & Quote

You receive a custom mood board and a transparent quote tailored exactly to your budget.
